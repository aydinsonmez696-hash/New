V3.3 - BRANCHING CANON (sandbox mode)

What it is
  Canon is the default future, not a promise. When your story kills someone early, or saves someone canon kills,
  every canon event, agenda line, news clause and story-card line that depends on them is dropped, its day stops
  being a skip stop, and its news is never delivered. The note's "Changed in this story" lines tell the AI what
  happened instead. Everything you never touched still happens on schedule. State lines (the Seven, Vought, the
  world) are kept and tagged "(In this story X is dead/alive)" so the AI sees the contradiction and plays around it.

  Also fixed: a reply's canon words can no longer move the date past the next canon stop. In v3.2, "On the news:
  A-Train killed a young woman, Robin Ward" on June 5, 2019 jumped the clock to February 2026.

Commands
  /canon           shows the mode (branch is the default for every adventure, old ones included)
  /canon strict    canon happens on its day whatever your story did (the v3.2 behavior)
  /canon branch    back to branching
  /status          now shows the mode too

How "depends on" is decided
  1. By name: a line that names someone the story changed, on or after the day of the change, is off-script.
  2. By an optional dependency on the row itself, for events whose cause the text doesn't name:
       CANON rows: a 6th element, e.g. "!Madelyn Stillwell" (her canon death must stand) or "Homelander" (alive).
       NEWS rows: a 10th element, same format. Seven items carry one (July 2019, June 2022, Nov 2024, Jan 2025,
       Feb/Mar/Apr 2026 all need Homelander; Jan 2025 and Apr 2026 also need Neuman / Butcher).
     Three CANON rows carry one so far (Ashley's promotion, Ryan going to Mallory, Calhoun's presidency). Add more
     where you know a chain: it's data, not code.

Known limits (by design, cheap to live with)
  - A news item is dropped by clause ("; " apart). A clause with a pronoun pointing at a dropped one can read oddly
    ("the FBI is hunting William Butcher for it" after Stillwell's clause is gone).
  - A line that merely mentions a changed person is dropped too (the Godolkin massacre news goes when Homelander is
    dead, because he arrests the students in it). Quieter beats contradictory.
  - The "needs" lists are judgment calls. Rows without one rely on names alone.

Install
  Paste "7 Script - Library v3.3.js" into Scripts > Library on your PRIVATE copy. Nothing else changes
  (AI Instructions, Plot Essentials, Author's Note, cards and the three hook scripts are the v3.2 ones).

Tests (from this folder, with node)
  node tests/tests-branch.js "7 Script - Library v3.3.js" "<v3.2 Library>" "<4 Plot Essentials.txt>" "<6 Story Cards.json>" tests/harness.js
  The v3.2 tests-lite and fuzz-lite also run against it unchanged.

Results
  tests-lite 69/69. tests-branch 35/35. fuzz-lite seed 12345: 2 notes over 10,000 chars in 4,800 turns (v3.2 had 1;
  same cause, a busy full-tier day, not the branching), seed 777: 0 failures. Hooks under 80 ms.
  Context: with nothing changed the note is byte-identical to v3.2. After three changes (Homelander killed 2010,
  A-Train killed 2016, Stillwell saved 2019) the note is smaller in branch than strict on every date tested, by
  257 to 2,154 chars (average -1,120), because dropped lines cost nothing. The persistent "Changed in this story"
  lines are the only ongoing cost: about 40-80 chars per change, as in v3.2.
